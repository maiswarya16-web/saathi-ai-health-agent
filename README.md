# Saathi AI Health Agent

An AI-powered multilingual assistant designed to support frontline health workers with reliable health information, screening guidance, referral support, and follow-up assistance.

## Problem

Frontline health workers such as ASHAs often work in communities where they need quick access to reliable health information and guidance.

Important information may be spread across training materials, guidelines, and different systems. This can make it difficult to quickly find appropriate information during field work.

## Our Solution

Saathi is an AI-powered assistant that helps frontline health workers:

- Find relevant health information quickly
- Understand health procedures in simple language
- Identify potential warning signs
- Support appropriate referral decisions
- Create follow-up reminders
- Interact using multiple Indian languages

## Target Users

- ASHA workers
- ANM workers
- Community health workers
- Other frontline healthcare workers

## Key Features

1. Multilingual AI assistant
2. Health information retrieval
3. Screening and warning-sign support
4. Referral guidance
5. Follow-up reminders
6. Simple conversational interface

## Safety

Saathi is designed as a decision-support and information tool. It does not replace qualified medical professionals or emergency medical services.
